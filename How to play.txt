1Click2Learn
============

How to play (Windows):
1. Unzip the whole folder first. Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click Play.bat.
3. The game opens in your web browser. Keep the black window open while you play.
   Close it when you're done.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
This happens because Play.bat was downloaded from the internet.

No internet connection or installation is needed.

You can also play online: https://keithk10.github.io/1click2learn/

Flash emulation by Ruffle (https://ruffle.rs).
